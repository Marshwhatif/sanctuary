How it works

  1. Tap Order

    It opens the order form on Ko-fi.

  2. Tell us about them

    In the same form, write your dog's name, their breed if you know it, and the background you'd like. Then tap Add attachment and add a photo (more angles help, but one is enough). Then pay.

  3. We confirm

    We check your photo and confirm your order within 2 days. If we can't make your dog from it, we'll ask for another photo or refund you in full.

  4. Meet your 3D dog

    We send you a look check. Tell us if anything needs changing, like a patch, the ears or the tail.

  5. Get your files

    Your finished portrait and wallpaper arrive by Ko-fi message or email.

good to know

Questions

Will it look exactly like my dog?

It will look like your dog in Marsh's blocky style: their colours, markings, ear shape, size and face, built from your photo. It's a cute 3D character rather than a photo-realistic copy, and you get a look check with one round of changes so you can get it right.

What if I only have one photo?

One clear photo is enough. Daylight, their whole body, and their face visible works best. Extra angles help us get the markings right.

Can I make one for a dog who has passed away?

Yes, and we'll take extra care with it. Old or blurry photos are fine. Send what you have.

Will my dog appear in a Marsh video or the sanctuary?

Not unless you ask. Your portrait is just for you. If you'd love your dog to appear on @marshwhatif, say so in the order form.

What can I do with my portrait?

Anything personal: your phone, your profile picture, posts, printing it for your wall, or giving it as a gift. Please don't sell it or put it on things you sell. We may show it as an example only if you've said yes.

Do you make cats or other pets?

No. We only make dogs, and we always will. Dogs are what Marsh knows best.

What happens to my photos?

We use them only to make your portrait. We delete them within 30 days of sending your finished files. See our privacy policy.

Can I get a refund?
  • Your portrait is made just for you, so once we've started it you can't cancel for a change of mind. When you order, you ask us to start straight away and agree that you lose your 14-day right to cancel once we begin.
  • We will refund you in full if we can't make your dog from your photos, if we haven't sent your look check within 14 days, or if your finished portrait isn't as described and we can't fix it.
  • To ask, message us on Ko-fi or DM @marshwhatif with your receipt. Your legal rights aren't affected.
I'm under 18. Can I order?

Please ask a parent or guardian first. They can order for you.
